Livecache-bench is a benchmarking system for evaluating wiki search quality. Referenced alongside the IDF self-tuning improvement applied from Metatron's three-tier retrieval design, where the benchmark validated that 10/10 queries with 17/17 items maintained 100% precision after adding IDF weighting to keyword ranking.
