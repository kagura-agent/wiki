The dna-preflight system surfaces reminders about DNA pattern violations at decision points. An applied insight from [[metatron-codebase-priors]] added recidivism detection: patterns surfaced 5+ times trigger a "structural problem" escalation, shifting from reminder to action. This closes the feedback loop so surfaced context is not just broadcast but its effectiveness is measured via a `.preflight-log`.
