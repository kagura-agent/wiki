# Distillation

Knowledge compression from experience or documentation into compact, actionable representations for agents. Closely related to [[domain-specific-compression]] — distillation selects what to keep, compression decides how to encode it. The output often feeds [[agent-memory-architecture]] or [[skill-compilation-pattern]] where runtime behavior is derived from distilled knowledge.
