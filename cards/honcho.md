Honcho is a dialectic user modeling platform for AI agents, providing personalized context and [[agent-memory]] through conversational modeling. It builds user representations by analyzing dialogue patterns rather than relying on static profiles. Agents connected to Honcho can adapt their behavior based on accumulated understanding of individual users, enabling more relevant and context-aware interactions over time. Related to [[intent-aware-retrieval]] and [[progressive-retrieval]] approaches for personalizing agent context.
