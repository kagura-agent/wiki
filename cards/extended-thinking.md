# Extended Thinking

LLM capability for longer [[reasoning]] chains through dedicated thinking blocks, becoming a provider-specific feature with compatibility implications. Extended thinking increases [[test-time-compute]] by allowing the model to deliberate before responding, and interacts with [[tool-calling]] since models must decide when to think versus when to act.
